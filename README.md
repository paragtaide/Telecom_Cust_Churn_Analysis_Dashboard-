# 📊 Telecom Customer Churn Analysis Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview
This project focuses on analyzing telecom customer churn data to identify the major reasons behind customer loss and provide actionable business recommendations. The dashboard helps businesses understand customer behavior, retention patterns, contract preferences, and risk categories using interactive visualizations.

The analysis was performed using Power BI to transform raw customer data into meaningful business insights that support data-driven decision-making.

---

# 🎯 Problem Statement
Telecom companies face significant revenue loss due to increasing customer churn. Understanding why customers leave is essential for improving retention strategies and customer satisfaction.

The goal of this project is to:
- Identify churn patterns
- Analyze customer behavior
- Detect high-risk customer segments
- Improve retention strategies
- Support business decision-making through visualization

# 🔑 Key Insights

## 📌 High Churn in Month-to-Month Contracts
Customers with short-term contracts have the highest churn rate.

### Business Insight:
Short-term customers are less loyal and more likely to switch providers.

---

## 📌 Customers Without Tech Support Churn More
Lack of technical support significantly increases customer churn.

### Business Insight:
Customer support quality directly impacts retention.

---

## 📌 New Customers Are More Likely to Leave
Customers with low tenure (0–12 months) show higher churn behavior.

### Business Insight:
Early customer experience plays a critical role in retention.

---

## 📌 High-Risk Customers Need Attention
A large number of customers fall into high-risk categories.

### Business Insight:
Predictive retention strategies are necessary to reduce future churn.
